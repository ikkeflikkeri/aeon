# Agent Displacement Tracker

*Last run: 2026-06-02*

## Known Displacement Events (baseline)
- Klarna (2024): replaced 700 customer support agents with AI. Support resolution time 2min vs 11min human avg. Headcount fell from 7,400 to ~3,000. By early 2026, quietly rebuilding after complex-case quality deterioration.
- Duolingo (2024): cut ~10% of contractors, cited AI content generation replacing human translators.
- Salesforce (2025): froze non-essential hiring across sales/support. Customer support cut from 9,000 to ~5,000 citing AI agent handle rate; hundreds reportedly redeployed rather than fully eliminated.
- IBM (2024–2025): eliminated ~8,000 positions, projecting $640M annual savings. Shift toward AI in data entry, customer service, entry-level programming.
- Oracle (March 2026): ~30,000 total layoffs (18% of ~162k workforce). 12,000 support and consulting roles directly replaced by internal AI agents and offshore partners. RHS and SVOS divisions lost ~30% each. $8–10B annual cash freed, redirected to AI data centers. Workers report being fired after training the models replacing them.
- Intuit (May 2026): 3,000 employees cut (17% of 18,200-person workforce). Stated rationale: "focus on AI efforts." No specific AI system named.
- Meta (May 2026): 8,000 employees cut (10% of workforce). AI efficiency push cited.
- ClickUp (May 2026): 290 employees cut (22% of 1,300-person workforce). Replaced by 3,000 internal AI agents — 3:1 agent:human ratio. Agents deployed across engineering, marketing, support, executive ops. CEO frames as "100x org" model.
